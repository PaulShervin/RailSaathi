# RailSathi AI — Session 011: Venue Geocoding & Station Resolution Pipeline Audit

- **Date**: 2026-10-10
- **Time**: 15:00 IST
- **Focus**: Venue Geocoding, Multi-Branch Disambiguation, Station Classification & Last-Mile Urban Routing

---

## 1. Audit Findings of Previous Implementation

1. **False Substring Matching**: Substring matching in `LocationResolutionService` caused queries like `"Tidel Park, Pattabiram"` to erroneously match `"Tidel Park"` in Chennai, returning Taramani coordinates (12.9893, 80.2483) rather than Pattabiram (13.1235, 80.0574), >25km away.
2. **Silent City-Centroid Hallucination**: Unverified venues defaulted to the Chennai Central centroid (13.0827, 80.2707) with low confidence rather than honestly reporting `latitude=None` and requesting clarification.
3. **Ambiguity Ignored**: Venues present in multiple cities across Tamil Nadu (TIDEL Park in Taramani, Pattabiram, Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy) did not prompt the user for clarification when no city was supplied.
4. **No Station Classification**: Station registry lacked classification into intercity terminals vs junctions vs suburban/MRTS stops.
5. **No Durable Location Caching**: Geocoding and routing were recomputed in-memory without persistent cache or TTLs.

---

## 2. Corrected Implementation & Deliverables

1. **Multi-Branch Disambiguation**:
   - Distinct verified records for `Tidel Park, Taramani, Chennai`, `TIDEL Park Pattabiram`, `TIDEL Park Coimbatore`, `TIDEL / ELCOT IT Park Madurai`, `TIDEL Park Trichy`.
   - Ambiguous queries without locality context trigger `requires_user_clarification=True` with candidate options and low confidence (0.40).
2. **Durable SQLite Caching (`LocationCacheManager`)**:
   - `geocoding_cache` (30-day TTL) and `routing_cache` (7-day TTL) in SQLite (`data/location_cache.sqlite`) with WAL mode.
   - 100% architecturally separated from RailRadar; 0 RailRadar API calls consumed for location resolution.
3. **Station Classification & Intercity Filtering**:
   - Stations categorized as `major_intercity_terminal`, `intercity_junction`, `intercity_halt`, `suburban_halt`, `metro`.
   - Pure MRTS stops (`TYMR`, `KTBR`) filtered out from long-distance intercity train searches.
4. **Deterministic Urban Traffic Routing Model**:
   - Distance modeled with 1.30 road detour factor over Haversine distance.
   - Travel duration: 22 km/h base speed + 8-minute taxi dispatch/station exit buffer. Unknown duration is never treated as zero.
5. **Station Selection Logic**:
   - Supports selecting a farther major terminal (`MAS`/`MS` vs `TBM`) when it provides a feasible direct schedule meeting the passenger's meeting deadline.
6. **Telemetry & Structured Response**:
   - Returns `selected_station`, `station_selection_reason`, `last_mile_distance`, `last_mile_duration_minutes`, `last_mile_source`, and `location_telemetry`.
